About Our Little World

Every little world has a reason for existing. This one began with something simple — a love for cute kitties, tiny details and the kind of moments that deserve to be remembered.

Instead of putting everything into one place, this website turns those thoughts into a small journey, with every page revealing another part of the story.

THE IDEA

More than just a website

This isn't meant to feel like a normal collection of web pages. The idea is to make the experience feel calm, personal and slightly playful — almost like opening a tiny box filled with handwritten notes.

That's why the kitty theme stays present throughout the whole journey. The colours, little paws, cards, spacing and animations will all belong to the same visual world.

THE STORY

Why are there different pages?

Because one page can never properly hold every thought, every memory and every little detail.

So the website is divided into small chapters. The Home page starts the journey, About explains the little world, the Letter gives space to personal words, Memories keeps the moments together and Surprise saves something special for the very end.

Each chapter is intentionally separated so the next page feels like discovering another little part of the same story.
