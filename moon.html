CHAPTER SIX

Under The Same Moon

Different places. One sky above us.

☾ UNDER THE SAME MOON ☽

Look up at the sky tonight.

Somewhere above you, the same moon is shining.

And somewhere above me, it is shining too.

We might be in completely different places, surrounded by different streets, different buildings and different sounds.

But the sky doesn't really care about the distance.

“Different places. One moon. One sky.”

I think there's something strangely comforting about that.

Knowing that even when we're not looking at the same view, there is still something above both of us.

The same moon.

The same stars.

The same night sky stretching across the distance.

✦ ✦ ✦

A Little Night Thought

Nights have a way of making ordinary thoughts feel a little more meaningful.

Everything gets quieter.

The day finally slows down.

And suddenly your mind starts wandering through all the little things you didn't think about during the day.

Sometimes that's when I think about you.

Not because anything dramatic happened.

Just because you're there somewhere in the back of my mind, like a tiny notification that never completely disappears.

“Some people become part of your thoughts without even trying.”

Maybe you're busy.

Maybe I'm busy.

Maybe neither of us is even thinking about the same thing.

And that's perfectly okay.

Still, somewhere underneath all the ordinary parts of the day, there can be a simple thought:

“I wonder how her day went.” “I hope she's doing okay.” “I should send her this.” “She'd probably laugh at this.”
✦

When The Night Gets Quiet

There is something peaceful about knowing that distance doesn't change the sky.

The moon doesn't stop at borders.

The stars don't ask which country you're in.

The night simply exists above both of us.

And maybe that gives us a tiny reminder:

“Being far away doesn't mean being completely apart.”

There can still be shared moments.

A song playing at night.

A message arriving when the day is finally quiet.

Looking at the moon for a few seconds and remembering someone who is somewhere else.

Tiny things.

But sometimes tiny things are exactly what make a connection feel special.

✦ ✦ ✦

A Sky We Can Both See

Maybe someday we'll look back at these nights and remember how far apart everything once felt.

The waiting.

The busy schedules.

The different time in different places.

The messages that started with “just one thing” and somehow became much longer conversations.

Maybe we'll laugh about how seriously we used to think about the distance.

Maybe we'll remember the nights when all we had to share was a screen.

And maybe one day the distance won't feel quite as important.

“Until then, the sky is still ours to look at.”
☾

For The Nights You Miss The Connection

If there is ever a night when everything feels a little too far away, remember that one quiet moment doesn't define the whole story.

Busy days happen.

Late replies happen.

Tired days happen.

Life happens.

But the little moments we've already shared don't disappear because one day was difficult or quiet.

The memories are still there.

The laughs are still there.

The conversations are still there.

And the little universe we created is still there too.

“The night can be quiet without the story being over.”
✦ ✦ ✦

One Small Wish

If I could make one small wish while looking at the moon, it wouldn't be for everything to suddenly become perfect.

It would simply be for more good moments.

More reasons to laugh.

More conversations that neither of us planned.

More ordinary days that somehow become memorable.

More little reasons to be grateful.

And hopefully, someday, a little less distance.

“Until someday, I'll just look up and remember: somewhere under this same moon, you're living your own little story too.”
✦ ✦ ✦

Goodnight, Little Moon

So whenever you see the moon glowing in the night sky, let it remind you of something simple.

There are two places.

There are two everyday lives.

There is a lot of distance.

But there is also one sky above us.

And somewhere between that sky, our messages and all our little moments, there is a tiny world that belongs to this chapter.

A world made of stars, moonlight, memories, conversations and a little bit of kitty chaos.

“Under the same moon, across the distance.”

From one little corner of the world
to another. ☾🤍
