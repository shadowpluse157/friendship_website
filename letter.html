A LETTER FOR YOU

Dear Ali 🩷

There are some things I wanted to say properly.

🐱
OUR LITTLE STORY 24 APRIL → 16 JULY

Dear Ali,

I honestly don't even know where to begin, because when I think about everything that happened between the beginning and now, it feels like there are a hundred little things I could talk about.

So maybe I'll start with the simplest thing:

I'm really glad I met you.

What started on 24 April could have stayed as just another ordinary meeting. We could have talked, laughed a little, and eventually gone our separate ways.

But somehow, that didn't happen.

Instead, one conversation became another. Then another. And before I knew it, talking to you had become one of those little things I actually looked forward to.

And honestly, I think that's one of my favourite things about our story.

It wasn't forced. It just slowly became important.

01
THE BFF CHAPTER

From Friends To My Favourite Person

Then we became BFFs.

And that sounds like such a simple sentence, but it really wasn't simple to me.

Because somewhere inside all those random conversations, jokes, little updates, teasing, and completely unnecessary discussions, I started realising that you weren't just someone I talked to.

You were becoming someone I trusted, someone I cared about, and someone whose presence genuinely mattered to me.

I started remembering the little things you said.

I started noticing when something was bothering you.

And I started wanting to know how your day was going, even when there wasn't anything particularly interesting to talk about.

That's when I think you started becoming more than just a friend.

02
THE IMPORTANT PERSON CHAPTER

Somehow, You Became Someone I Couldn't Ignore

There came a point where calling you "just my friend" didn't really explain how important you had become.

You had your own little place in my thoughts and in my day.

Even a normal message from you could completely change my mood.

And yes, sometimes I probably acted completely ridiculous about it. 😭

But that's because I cared.

I cared about your happiness. I cared when you were stressed. I cared when you were busy. I cared when something made you sad.

And most importantly, I cared about the person behind all those messages.

You became important without asking to become important.

03
THE CRUSH CHAPTER

And Then My Feelings Got A Little Obvious

And then, somewhere along the way, things started feeling a little different.

I started looking forward to your messages a little too much.

I started smiling at the screen for absolutely no reason whenever your name appeared.

And sometimes I would see something funny or cute and immediately think, "I need to show Ali this." 😭🩷

That's when I realised that maybe this wasn't just friendship anymore.

You had become my crush.

And honestly, having a crush on someone who was already one of my closest people made everything feel even more special.

Because I didn't just like the idea of you.

I already knew the person I was falling for.

04
THE LOVERS CHAPTER

Somehow, We Became Us

Then came the part of our story where everything became a little more real.

The feelings weren't just something quietly sitting in the background anymore.

There was something between us that felt different from before.

Something warm.

Something comforting.

Something that made me think, "Yeah... I really don't want to lose this."

And then came 16 July.

16
JULY OUR SPECIAL DAY
🩷

You asked me to be your boyfriend.

And I said yes.

Just like that, our story got another chapter.

But honestly, "just like that" doesn't really describe how special that moment felt to me.

Because it wasn't just about becoming a couple.

It was about everything that came before it.

The friendship.

The trust.

The conversations.

The feelings.

All those little moments eventually led us there.

And that's why 16 July isn't just another date to me.

It's a reminder of how unexpectedly beautiful our story became.

05
ABOUT US

It's The Little Things

You know what I think I like most?

It's not one giant dramatic moment.

It's all the tiny things.

🩷

The random conversations that somehow turn into hours.

🐱

The silly moments that would probably make no sense to anyone else.

✨

The way a simple message from you can make an ordinary day feel better.

🌸

The fact that we can be serious one minute and completely ridiculous the next.

🐾

And all those little inside moments that slowly became ours.

Those are the things I want to remember.

Not just the big dates. The little moments too.

06
OUR FUTURE

And I Hope This Isn't The Last Chapter

And now I want to talk about something I think about sometimes.

The future.

I don't know exactly what it will look like.

None of us really can.

Life changes. People grow. Plans change. New chapters appear when we least expect them.

But there's one thing I hope for.

I hope you are still somewhere in my story.

I want to see us grow as people.

I want to look back at this little website one day and laugh at how cute and dramatic we were.

I want there to be new memories that aren't written here yet.

New jokes.

New conversations.

New celebrations.

New challenges that we somehow get through.

And hopefully a ridiculous number of little moments that make us say, "Remember when...?"

🌷

More Memories

More moments worth remembering.

✨

More Growth

Becoming better versions of ourselves.

🩷

More Us

More chapters in whatever beautiful way life brings them.

I don't want to promise you some unrealistic perfect future.

I just want something more genuine.

Whatever life brings, I hope we keep choosing kindness, honesty, patience, and each other.

And if someday we look back at where everything started, I hope we'll remember that it all began with something as simple as two people meeting on 24 April.

🔖
A NOTE FOR OUR FUTURE SELVES

If you're reading this years from now, I hope you're smiling. I hope life treated you kindly. And I hope we never forgot how special these little beginnings were.

07
ONE MORE THING

Thank You, Ali

Thank you for becoming such an important part of my life.

Thank you for all the conversations, the laughs, the random moments, the patience, and all the little memories we've collected along the way.

Thank you for being someone I can genuinely care about.

And thank you for giving our story a chance to become what it is now.

I don't think you realise how special you are to me.

Sometimes I probably don't say it properly.

Sometimes I joke around instead of saying something serious.

Sometimes I act like I don't care just to tease you.

But underneath all of that, I genuinely care about you a lot.

🐱 A LITTLE CONFESSION 🩷

You Have A Slight Problem...

You're becoming way too easy to miss.

And honestly? I blame you. 😭🩷

You somehow managed to become one of my favourite notifications, one of my favourite conversations, and one of my favourite people.

Case closed. You're stuck with me. 🐾
♡

If I could go back to 24 April and tell that version of me everything that would happen, I probably wouldn't believe it.

I wouldn't believe that the person I was meeting that day would eventually become my BFF, then my important person, then my crush, then someone I fell for, and finally my girlfriend/boyfriend chapter on 16 July.

Life is weird like that.

Sometimes the best stories are the ones you never planned.

And I'm really happy that somehow, ours became one of them.

✨
MY WISH FOR YOU

I Hope You Win In Life

I hope you achieve every dream you've been working towards.

I hope you become the person you've always wanted to be.

I hope you rise above every challenge that comes your way.

I hope you find reasons to smile even on difficult days.

And more than anything, I hope you never stop believing in yourself.

Because you deserve good things. 🩷

So, Ali...

Thank you for being you.

Thank you for being part of my story.

And here's to all the chapters we haven't written yet.

Yours, 🩷

End of this letter... but definitely not the end of our story.
