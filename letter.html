A letter from the heart

For Ali

Some things are too special to squeeze into a short message. So this time, I wanted to give my words a little more room.

🐾 ♡ 🐾
PERSONAL LETTER 🐾
🐱
♡

Open this slowly.

No need to rush through it. Every little paragraph has a reason for being here.

🐾

Written especially for you.

Dear Ali, With a little extra love

I don't really know where to begin, because whenever I try to put everything I feel into words, it somehow feels like there are still a hundred things left unsaid.

But maybe that's okay. Maybe a letter doesn't have to explain everything perfectly. Maybe it just needs to be honest.

So here I am, writing this little piece of my heart and hoping that when you read it, you can feel the meaning behind the words instead of only reading them.

And yes, I know this whole website is filled with kitties. 🐱 But honestly, that felt right. Something about kitties being soft, slightly silly, comforting and impossible not to smile at made them the perfect little theme for this place.

🐾 Yours,
♡
SOMETHING I WANT YOU TO KNOW

You became important without even trying.

That's probably one of the sweetest things about it.

There wasn't some dramatic moment where everything suddenly changed. It was little things. Conversations. Random messages. Laughing at completely stupid things. Waiting for a reply. Remembering tiny details.

And somewhere between all those ordinary moments, you became someone whose presence started to matter a little more than I expected.

Someone I look forward to hearing from. Someone whose messages can change the mood of an ordinary day. Someone who can make me smile just by appearing on my screen.

🐱
THE THINGS I ADMIRE

There are so many little things about you that I adore.

🐱

Your little reactions

The way you react to things, the random little expressions, the silly moments — somehow they always manage to make you even more adorable.

🤍

Your presence

Even when we aren't saying anything particularly important, having you around can still make an ordinary moment feel different.

✨

The way you make me smile

Sometimes one message from you is enough to completely change the mood of my day. I don't think you realise how powerful that little effect is.

🐾 A LITTLE CONFESSION 🐾

You are dangerously cute, you know that? 🤭

I swear sometimes you don't even have to do anything. You can simply exist and somehow my brain goes: "Yep. Still adorable."

And then I try to act normal about it, which obviously doesn't work very well. 😭

If being cute was illegal, I genuinely think you'd have a very serious problem by now.

And before you start smiling at this, yes — I'm blaming the kitties for making me this soft. 🐱

🐱 🤍
🐱
REMEMBER THIS You matter.

More than you probably realise.

FROM MY HEART

I hope you always know how much you mean to me.

I don't want this letter to only be cute. I want it to be honest too.

There will be days when everything feels easy and there will probably be days when life becomes stressful, confusing or exhausting.

On those difficult days, I hope you never feel like you have to pretend that everything is perfect. You can be tired. You can have a bad day. You can be quiet. You can need some space.

None of those things make you less important.

And if you ever forget how special you are, I hope this little website can remind you that somewhere in this tiny kitty corner of the internet, there are words written especially with you in mind.

🐾 ♡ 🐾
OKAY... ONE FLIRTY PART 🤭

Don't blame me. You made me write this.

🐱

Stop being so cute.

It's becoming unfair.
🤭

Why are you this adorable?

I'm trying to concentrate here.
♡

If I keep smiling at my screen, I'm blaming you.

Completely your fault.
A FEW FUTURE WISHES

Things I hope we get to experience.

Not promises about exactly how life will turn out — just little hopes I'd love to carry forward.

🌙

Late-night conversations

I hope there are plenty more nights where we forget what time it is because we're busy talking about completely random things.

☕

Peaceful days

I hope life gives you many peaceful days where you can simply relax, smile and feel proud of how far you've come.

✈️

New experiences

New places, new memories, new stories, unexpected adventures and lots of moments we'll eventually look back on and laugh about.

🐾

More kitty moments

Obviously this one is mandatory. A future without enough kitty content simply isn't acceptable. 🐱

✦
🐾
SOMEDAY

I hope we look back at this and smile.

Maybe one day we'll look back at this little website and laugh about how ridiculously much effort went into making a kitty world.

And maybe we'll remember all the little conversations, the silly jokes, the awkward moments, the good days and everything in between.

That's one of my favourite thoughts about the future — not knowing exactly what it will look like, but knowing there are still so many moments waiting to happen.

🐱 ♡ 🐾
🐱
LITTLE PROMISES

Things I genuinely want to remember.

These aren't dramatic promises about controlling the future. They're simple reminders about how I want to treat the person I care about.

🐾 🐾 🐾
MY PROMISES

A few things I want to promise you.

Simple promises. Honest ones. The kind that matter more than perfectly written words.

01
🤍

I'll listen.

When you have something to say, I'll try to listen instead of simply waiting for my turn to speak.

02
🐾

I'll respect your space.

Caring about someone also means understanding that they sometimes need their own time, their own space and their own quiet moments.

03
🌙

I'll be honest with you.

Even when something is difficult to explain, I'll always value honesty instead of pretending everything is fine.

04
✨

I'll appreciate the little things.

The random messages, silly jokes, tiny updates and unexpected moments deserve to be appreciated too.

05
🐱

I'll keep the kitty spirit alive.

This one is absolutely non-negotiable. There will always be room for cats, paws and ridiculous amounts of cuteness.

06
♡

I'll keep choosing kindness.

No matter how busy life becomes, kindness should never become something we forget about.

FOR THE FUTURE
🐱

I don't need to know everything about tomorrow.

I just hope tomorrow has reasons for you to smile.

I hope you get the things you've been working for. I hope your dreams become plans, your plans become memories and your memories become stories you are proud to tell.

I hope you get peaceful mornings, exciting days, comfortable evenings and people around you who genuinely appreciate you.

And if life ever becomes difficult, I hope you remember that difficult chapters are still chapters — not the whole story.

🐾

Whatever the future looks like, I hope it gives you plenty of reasons to be happy.

🐾
♡
ONE MORE THING

If you ever wonder whether I care...

Look at all these words.

I could have written a tiny message. I could have said something simple and moved on.

Instead, I sat here building an entire little kitty world and filling it with words for you.

So I think that answers the question pretty well. 🐱

You matter to me.

🐱 🤍 🐾
🐾 END OF THE LETTER 🐾

Dear Ali,

If you've actually made it this far, then congratulations — you have officially survived one extremely long letter. 😭🐱

But honestly, I hope you didn't just read the words. I hope you felt the thought behind them.

Every section was written because I wanted this little website to feel like something more than just another webpage.

I wanted it to feel like a small place you could visit and immediately know: "Someone made this with me in mind."

And yes, I know I probably got a little carried away with the kitties.

But can you really complain when they're this cute? 🤭🐱

With lots of care, Your favourite kitty-person 🐾
