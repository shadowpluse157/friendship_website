So I decided to put them here.

A little letter ♡
Dear

Ali

Hey Ali,

I honestly don't even know where to start with this letter.

There are some things that are easy to explain. You can put them into one sentence, one message, or one simple answer and move on.

But then there are people who somehow become connected to so many little moments that explaining what they mean becomes almost impossible.

And that's exactly why I ended up making this entire little website instead of just sending you a normal message.

Because a normal message didn't feel like enough.

✦

Some people deserve more than a few lines in a chat.

✦

I wanted to make something that you could actually explore.

Something where every page had a reason to exist.

Something that felt a little more personal than just typing, "hey, I made you a website."

So here we are.

A whole little corner of the internet, made especially for you.

🐾
♡
🐾

Where it all started

I still remember how strange it is that sometimes a person can enter your life so casually, without either of you knowing how many memories are going to come after that first conversation.

At first, it's just another conversation. Another notification. Another person on the other side of the screen.

And then, slowly, that person becomes someone whose messages you actually look forward to.

Someone whose absence you notice.

Someone whose random messages can completely change the mood of your day.

Somehow, that's what happened with you.

🐱
ONE THING I WANT YOU TO KNOW

You became important to me without even trying.

When we first met on 24 April, I had no idea that one conversation would eventually become such a big part of my life.

We went from meeting each other, to becoming best friends, to becoming people who could understand each other's random moods, jokes, busy days and completely chaotic conversations.

And honestly, that's something I'm really grateful for.

🐾

Some friendships become memories. Ours became a whole story.

🐾

There were funny moments, confusing moments, busy days, random conversations and probably enough cat-related things to fill an entire website.

Which is exactly why this website exists.

I wanted you to have something that says, in its own little way: "Hey, you matter."

For the person who somehow became one of my favourite parts of the story. ♡
THE LITTLE THINGS

It's always the little things.

If someone asked me what exactly made this friendship special, I don't think I could give them one perfect answer.

It wasn't one giant moment.

It was all the small things that slowly started adding up.

The random messages. The completely unnecessary conversations. The moments when neither of us really had anything important to say, but somehow we still ended up talking.

The jokes that probably wouldn't make sense to anyone else. The random cat pictures. The silly conversations. The days when one of us was busy and the other was waiting for a reply.

Even the ordinary moments became memorable simply because they were part of our story.

“

Sometimes the smallest moments become the memories you remember the longest.

”
AND THEN THERE ARE THE CATS

We obviously needed cats.

You love cats, I love cats, and honestly that was probably enough reason to turn this entire website into a tiny cat universe.

Every time I worked on this website, I kept thinking about how funny it would be if there were somehow cats everywhere.

So instead of fighting the idea, I just accepted it.

🐾 Cat approved. Obviously.
♡
THANK YOU

Thank you for being part of my story.

I don't always know how to say these things properly in normal conversations.

Sometimes I joke around instead. Sometimes I send something completely random. Sometimes I just disappear into my own work and come back later.

But underneath all of that, I genuinely appreciate having you in my life.

Thank you for the conversations, the laughs, the memories and all the completely random moments that somehow became important.

And thank you for making 24 April a date that I actually remember.

🐾

But we're not at the end yet...

🐾
ONE LAST THING

When you look back at this someday...

I hope you don't only remember the website.

I hope you remember why it was made.

Then going back and changing it again.

And again.

And probably again. 😭

💻
BEHIND THE CODE

Every page has a reason.

The home page is here to welcome you.

The about page is here because your story deserved its own space.

The memories page is for all those little moments that shouldn't disappear into an endless chat history.

And this letter exists because sometimes a normal message simply isn't enough.

✦

Even with distance between us...

One of the strange things about a long-distance connection is that you can be nowhere near someone and still have them become part of your everyday thoughts.

Different places. Different schedules. Different things happening throughout the day.

Yet somehow, a simple notification can make that distance feel a little smaller.

That's something I think is worth remembering.

🌙

Different places don't erase meaningful memories.

🌎
FROM HERE

This isn't really an ending.

There are still so many pages that haven't been written yet.

More conversations. More ridiculous jokes. More random screenshots. More cat pictures. More moments that will probably seem completely ordinary at first and become memories later.

I don't know exactly what the future will look like, and I don't think anyone really does.

But I do know that I'm glad our story reached this point.

🐾

Keep this little corner of the internet.

Not because a website can capture everything, because it can't.

But because sometimes it's nice to have somewhere you can return to and remember how everything started.

24 April — the beginning of a story worth remembering. ♡

🐱

And now...

🐾
🐱
BEFORE YOU LEAVE

If you ever wonder why I made this...

The answer is actually pretty simple.

I wanted to make something instead of just sending another ordinary message.

Something you could open later and still see a little piece of the time, effort and memories that went into it.

Maybe it's not the biggest or most perfect website in the world.

But every page has something behind it.

Every little detail was chosen for a reason.

🐾 ✦ ✧ ✦ 🐾

To Ali

Thank you for becoming part of my story.

Thank you for every laugh, every conversation, every random moment and every memory we've collected since 24 April.

Whatever happens next, I hope you always remember that this chapter mattered.

You are an important part of a story I will always be glad I started.
