A LITTLE NOTE 🐾
written with a little kitty magic ♡

Dear Ali,

I honestly don't know where to start, because somehow a simple little message can never explain everything I feel when I think about you.

You became one of those people who can turn an ordinary day into something I actually want to remember.

And maybe that's why I wanted to make this little world for you. Not because it had to be perfect. But because I wanted there to be something that says:

🐾 "You matter to me."

I know life gets busy. There are projects, responsibilities, work, random things that suddenly need your attention and days when everything feels like too much.

I've never wanted to be another thing you have to worry about. I just like hearing from you. Even a tiny message. Even something completely random. Somehow, the smallest things from you can mean a lot to me.

And yes... I know I can be a little dramatic sometimes. 😭 But that's part of the fun, isn't it?

Somewhere between the random conversations, the jokes, the teasing, the cute moments and all those tiny conversations we probably wouldn't explain to anyone else... you became someone really special to me.

🐾 ♡ 🐾

And this is only the beginning of the letter.

🐾 LITTLE THINGS

Things about you that I quietly adore.

Maybe you don't notice all of them, but I do.

01 🐱

Your cute side

You somehow manage to be adorable without even trying. And honestly, sometimes I wonder if you know the effect you have on me.

02 💬

Our random conversations

From completely random topics to teasing each other for no reason, somehow even the most ordinary conversations become memorable.

03 🐾

The kitty connection

Maybe this whole kitty world was inevitable. We both like cats, so obviously our little universe needed paws everywhere.

04 ♡

The way you understand me

Even when life gets complicated, being understood by someone makes everything feel a little lighter.

05 ✦

Your little surprises

Sometimes it's just a message, a joke or a tiny moment I didn't expect. Those are the things that stay in my mind.

06 😭

Even your teasing

I complain about it, pretend to be annoyed, and then secretly enjoy it anyway.

🐾
A VERY IMPORTANT OBSERVATION

You should probably stop being this cute.

Actually... don't. I think I've gotten way too used to it.

♡
SOMETHING I REALLY WANT YOU TO KNOW

I'm glad our paths crossed.

Out of all the people we could have met, somehow our conversations found their way to each other.

And I don't take that for granted.

I don't know what every tomorrow will look like, and I don't want to pretend that I can predict everything.

But I do know that right now, having you in my life is something I'm genuinely grateful for.

So whenever you see this little page, I hope you remember that somewhere behind all the jokes, teasing and ridiculous kitty energy...

🐾 someone really cares about you. ♡

Someone worth laughing with. Someone worth remembering. Someone whose presence can make an ordinary day feel different.

🐾 You deserve to know your own worth. 🐾

ONE LAST THING 🐾

Ali,

If you ever wonder why I made an entire little website instead of just sending you a message, this is probably the simplest answer I can give:

“

Some people are worth making a little extra effort for.

”

You're one of those people for me.

I appreciate the conversations, the jokes, the teasing, the random moments and even the little things that probably seem completely normal to you.

They aren't completely normal to me. They're pieces of a story I genuinely like having.

So whenever you come back to this page, I hope it doesn't feel like just another website.

I hope it feels like a tiny reminder that someone thought:

🐾 “Let's make something beautiful for Ali.” ♡

And that's exactly what this little kitty world is.

A small place made with a lot of care.

With lots of care, Your favourite kitty person ♡
