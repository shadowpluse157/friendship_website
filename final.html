CHAPTER TEN

Something Hidden

Not everything special has to be obvious at first.

✦ SOMETHING HIDDEN ✦

If you made it this far...

then you probably already know that this website wasn't supposed to be just a normal birthday page.

It was supposed to feel like opening a little world, one chapter at a time.

“Some of the best things are hidden between the obvious ones.”

So before the final chapter, I wanted to leave one little secret here.

Not a complicated secret.

Not something you have to solve.

Just something I wanted you to notice.

✦ ✦ ✦

Look A Little Closer

Every page you've seen so far was made around one simple idea:

“The little things matter.”

The stars.

The moon.

The music.

The distance.

The kitty moments.

The memories.

Even the little words hidden between the paragraphs.

They are all pieces of the same idea.

This website isn't really about the pages.

It's about the person reading them.

✦

The Little Secret

Here's the secret:

✦

You were the reason this entire little universe exists.

✦

Every page had to lead somewhere.

Every chapter needed a reason.

Every little detail had to belong here.

And the reason was simply to make one ordinary birthday feel a little less ordinary.

“The website is made from code, but the reason behind it is simple.”
✦ ✦ ✦

Something You Might Not Know

While making all of this, I kept thinking about how strange it is that something as simple as a website can hold so many little thoughts.

A normal webpage usually gives you information.

This one was meant to give you a feeling.

A little surprise.

A little smile.

Maybe a moment where you stop scrolling and just look around.

Maybe even a tiny, “Wait... this is actually cute.”

“Sometimes the best gift is simply knowing someone took the time.”
✦

The Hidden Message

If you remember only one thing from this entire website, let it be this:

YOU ARE IMPORTANT.

Not because you have to be perfect.

Not because every day has to be amazing.

Not because everything always has to go according to plan.

Simply because you are you.

✦ ✦ ✦

A Tiny Reminder

On busy days, remember that you don't have to accomplish everything at once.

On difficult days, remember that one difficult day doesn't define everything.

On ordinary days, remember that ordinary days can still contain something good.

And on happy days...

enjoy them.

Keep the little moments.

Save the silly memories.

Laugh at the ridiculous things.

And never underestimate how much a tiny moment can mean.

“A small moment can still deserve a big place in your memory.”
✦

Before The Final Chapter

There's only one more main page after this.

The final chapter.

The place where all these little pieces come together.

So take one last look around this page.

The stars.

The words.

The little hidden message.

And remember that every single part was placed here intentionally.

“One more chapter. Then the final gift.”
✦ ✦ ✦

And if you somehow thought this was the secret...

well...

maybe there are still a few surprises left.

But you'll have to keep going.

A little secret,
hidden between the stars. ✦🤍
