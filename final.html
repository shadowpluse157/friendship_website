Hey, Ali. 🤍

If you're reading this, you made it all the way to the last page.

What started as a little idea became this entire collection of pages, stars, words, music, memories, tiny surprises, and probably way too much kitty energy.

And somehow, after all of that, I still feel like there aren't enough words to say what I wanted this little website to say.

WHY I MADE THIS

More Than Just A Website

I didn't want this to feel like just another birthday message that appears on a screen and disappears after a few seconds.

I wanted to make something you could move through. Something with little chapters. Something that slowly tells a story.

A story about distance, little conversations, random moments, quiet nights, music, kitties, memories, and the people who can make ordinary days feel a little more special.
