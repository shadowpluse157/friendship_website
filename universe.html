CHAPTER FOUR

Our Little Universe

Some connections become their own little universe.

✦ OUR LITTLE UNIVERSE ✦

Somewhere between all the conversations, random messages, silly moments and ordinary days, we somehow created a little universe of our own.

It isn't a real place on any map. You won't find it by searching coordinates. It exists somewhere between two screens, two cities, two busy lives and all the little moments we choose to share.

And honestly, I think that's what makes it special.

“Two places can still feel like one little world.”

Your world is in Dubai. Mine is here in Pakistan. We have different routines, different responsibilities and sometimes completely different days.

But somehow there is still a small space where our worlds meet.

Sometimes that space is a conversation. Sometimes it's a random message. Sometimes it's a silly joke that would probably make no sense to anyone else.

Sometimes it's just a tiny thought during an ordinary day:

“I should tell her this.” “She would probably laugh at this.” “This reminds me of her.” “I need to send her this.”

And somehow those tiny things are enough to make an ordinary day feel a little less ordinary.

✦ ✦ ✦

A Universe Made Of Little Things

I don't think a connection needs huge moments all the time.

Sometimes the smallest things become the memories we remember the most.

A random conversation that lasted longer than expected. A message sent at exactly the right time. A joke that made absolutely no sense. A little update about the day. A completely unnecessary kitty picture.

None of these things sound enormous on their own.

But together, they become something.

They become our little collection of moments.

“Maybe a universe doesn't have to be enormous to mean everything.”

I like the idea that our little universe doesn't need to be perfect.

It can have quiet days. Busy days. Funny days. Random days. Days where we talk for hours and days where life gets in the way.

And that's okay.

Because the universe doesn't disappear just because one night is quiet.

✦

Our Two Little Corners

Somewhere far away, there is a girl in Dubai living her own everyday life.

And somewhere here in Pakistan, there is someone who can randomly think about that girl during the most ordinary moments.

Different places. Different routines. Different skies.

Yet somehow, the same little universe.

That's one of my favorite things about us.

We don't have to be in the same place for a moment to matter.

A message can cross the distance. A laugh can cross the distance. A memory can cross the distance. Even a simple “goodnight” can make two very different places feel just a little closer.

“The distance is real, but so is the connection.”

And maybe that's what this entire little website is trying to say.

This isn't just a collection of pages.

It's a tiny universe built from memories, wishes, words, stars, music and a ridiculous amount of kitty energy.

A universe made especially for one person.

✦ ✦ ✦

Keep This Little Universe

I hope that whenever you come back to this website, even years from now, it reminds you of this little chapter of our lives.

Not because everything was perfect.

But because it was real.

Two people. Two places. A lot of distance. A lot of busy days. And somehow, a little universe in between.

So if you ever forget how this little universe started, remember this:

“Two places • One little world.”

Made of little moments,
under the same sky. ✦
