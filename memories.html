CHAPTER NINE

Little Moments

The little things become the memories we keep.

✦ LITTLE MOMENTS ✦

Not every memory starts with something extraordinary.

Sometimes it starts with a completely normal conversation.

A random message.

A silly joke.

A song.

A picture.

Or a tiny moment that seemed unimportant at the time.

Then somehow, later, you remember it.

“The smallest moments sometimes leave the longest memories.”

That's what this chapter is about.

Not one huge event.

Just the little pieces that slowly become part of a story.

✦ ✦ ✦

The Conversations

There have been conversations that lasted much longer than either of us expected.

The kind that starts with one completely normal topic and somehow ends somewhere completely different.

One minute you're talking about your day.

Then you're talking about something random.

Then you're laughing about something that probably wouldn't even make sense to anyone else.

And suddenly you realise you've been talking for way longer than planned.

“Just five minutes somehow becomes an entire conversation.”

Those are the moments I like.

The ones that don't need to be planned.

✦

The Random Things

Then there are all the completely random things.

The messages that arrive with absolutely no warning.

The random pictures.

The little updates about what happened during the day.

The things that seem too small to mention, but somehow still get mentioned anyway.

I think those things matter more than we realise.

Because when someone becomes part of your everyday thoughts, even the smallest things start feeling worth sharing.

“If something makes you think of someone, suddenly it becomes worth sending.”
✦ ✦ ✦

The Little Laughs

Some memories aren't really about what happened.

They're about how something made you feel.

A stupid joke.

A completely unnecessary reaction.

A moment where neither person could stop laughing.

Something that would sound ridiculous if explained to someone else.

But that's okay.

Not every memory needs an explanation.

“Some jokes are only funny because they're ours.”
✦

The Busy Days

Not every memory is a long conversation.

Sometimes it's simply a message during a busy day.

A quick check-in.

A “good morning.”

A “goodnight.”

A tiny reminder that someone is still there, even while life is happening around them.

Those little moments can be easy to overlook.

But sometimes they're the ones that quietly matter the most.

“Being busy doesn't erase the little ways we show that we care.”
✦ ✦ ✦

Things I Hope We Remember

I hope we remember the ordinary days.

The days where nothing huge happened, but we still found a reason to talk.

I hope we remember the random conversations.

The silly jokes.

The unexpected messages.

The nights where we kept talking longer than planned.

The little moments that seemed normal at the time but became special later.

“Maybe memories are just ordinary moments that we decided to keep.”
✦

More Moments Ahead

The best part about memories is that they don't have to stop.

There are still so many ordinary days ahead.

More conversations.

More random messages.

More birthdays.

More songs.

More silly moments.

More reasons to laugh.

More things we haven't even experienced yet.

And maybe someday, we'll look back at this exact chapter and realise it was only the beginning of a much bigger collection of memories.

“The next memory hasn't happened yet.”
✦ ✦ ✦

Keep The Little Things

If there's one thing I want this page to remind you of, it's this:

Don't underestimate the little things.

A short message can matter.

A random laugh can matter.

A few minutes can matter.

A tiny moment can become a memory.

And sometimes a memory becomes one of the reasons a person smiles years later.

“Keep the little moments. They become the story.”
✦ ✦ ✦

So here's to all the moments we've already had.

And here's to all the ones we haven't made yet.

The big ones.

The tiny ones.

The planned ones.

And especially the completely random ones.

Here's to the little moments
that quietly become everything. ✦🤍
