FROM MY MEMORY TO YOURS

Ali,

If you ever wonder why I made this page without putting a single picture on it, this is why.

I didn't want to make a page that only showed what we looked like or where we were.

I wanted to make a page that showed what I remember.

The conversations. The feelings. The random moments. The little changes. The dates that became important.

Because sometimes the smallest moments end up becoming the memories we keep the longest.

"Some memories live in photographs. Ours live in the little moments."

And honestly, I hope we keep collecting those moments.

Not because everything has to be perfect.

But because even the ordinary days can become special when the right person is part of them.

So here's to everything we've already remembered... and everything we haven't experienced yet. 🩷

Always a little dramatic, but always genuine. 🐱
