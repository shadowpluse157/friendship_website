My Dearest...

If you're reading this... then you've reached the most important part of this little journey.

I don't always know the perfect words to say... but I wanted this website to speak for my heart.

Every conversation, every laugh, every little moment we spend together... becomes a memory that I quietly keep.

Maybe I don't always express it perfectly, but spending time with you has become one of my favourite parts of every day.

There are days when I smile for no reason... then I realise the reason has always been you.

Thank you for every smile you've given me, every moment you've stayed with me, and every memory we've created together.

Even on busy days, even when life becomes complicated, you're still someone I always want to make time for.

You may never realise how much your presence changes my day, but trust me... it does.

Sometimes I get quiet, sometimes I overthink, and sometimes I miss you more than I know how to explain.

But no matter what... my feelings for you never change.

You don't have to be perfect to be special to me.

I don't love you because you're perfect... I love you because you're you.

Every little thing about you, your smile, your kindness, your laugh, your presence... has slowly become a part of my happiness.

If I had to choose my favourite place... it would always be wherever you are.

Thank you for existing. Thank you for every moment. And thank you for being one of the best things that ever happened to me.

Happy Girlfriend Day. I Love You. Always. 🤍
