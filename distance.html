CHAPTER FIVE

Two Places • One Little World

Distance can separate places without separating a connection.

✦ TWO PLACES • ONE LITTLE WORLD ✦

There are two places in this little story.

Dubai.

Pakistan.

Two places that can feel incredibly far apart, especially when you wish you could simply be in the same place at the same time.

But distance is a strange thing.

It can tell us how many kilometres are between two places, but it can't really measure how much a person can mean to you.

“A map can measure distance, but it can't measure connection.”

You're in Dubai. I'm here in Pakistan.

Between those two places there are airports, roads, clouds, borders, different schedules and countless kilometres.

But there are also messages.

There are conversations.

There are memories.

And there are all those tiny moments that somehow make the distance feel a little smaller.

✦ ✦ ✦

The Distance Between Our Days

Sometimes distance isn't even about the places.

Sometimes it's simply about timing.

One person is busy while the other is free. One is working while the other is resting. One is having a normal day while the other is already thinking about tomorrow.

And sometimes both people are simply exhausted.

That's life.

We both have things to do. Responsibilities. Work. Studies. Family. Plans. Ordinary little things that fill up a day.

So I don't want distance to become something we constantly fight against.

I'd rather think of it as something we learn to understand.

Some days we get more time.

Some days we get less.

But even a small moment can still matter.

“It doesn't always have to be a lot. Sometimes a little is enough.”
✦

Messages That Cross The Distance

One of my favorite things about distance is also one of the simplest.

A message can travel farther than we can.

I can be sitting here in Pakistan, type something on a screen, and somehow those words reach you in Dubai.

That's pretty amazing when you actually think about it.

A tiny notification can connect two completely different parts of the world.

A simple “good morning” can cross borders.

A “how was your day?” can cross the distance.

A silly kitty picture can somehow travel hundreds of kilometres just to make someone smile.

And maybe that's why little messages can become such big parts of our days.

Dubai 🇦🇪 ───────── ✦ ───────── Pakistan 🇵🇰
✦ ✦ ✦

When The Distance Feels Bigger

Of course, distance isn't always easy.

There can be days when it feels annoying.

Days when you wish you didn't have to wait for a reply.

Days when you wish schedules matched perfectly.

Days when you wish you could simply say, “Come on, let's go somewhere,” instead of figuring out time zones, plans and everything else around it.

And that's okay.

Missing a connection doesn't make it weak.

Wanting a little more time doesn't make the little time you already have meaningless.

Sometimes it simply means someone matters.

“The hard days don't erase the good ones.”
✦

Making The Distance Smaller

We may not be able to control the kilometres between us.

But we can control the little things we do with the distance.

We can communicate.

We can be patient.

We can respect each other's busy days.

We can appreciate the moments when we do get time.

We can keep creating memories even when those memories happen through a screen.

And we can keep giving the little moments a place in our everyday lives.

Maybe that's how distance slowly becomes less intimidating.

Not because the kilometres disappear, but because the connection becomes stronger than the number.

“Two places. One little world. And countless little moments in between.”
✦ ✦ ✦

Someday

I don't know exactly what the future will look like.

Nobody really does.

But I hope there comes a day when we don't have to think about the distance quite as much.

A day when Dubai and Pakistan are simply two places on a map, instead of two places that make a connection feel far away.

Until then, there are still conversations to have, memories to make, jokes to laugh at, random things to share and plenty of kitty energy to survive.

And honestly...

That's enough for this little chapter.

“Until someday, we'll keep meeting somewhere between the messages.”

From one side of the distance
to the other. 🤍
