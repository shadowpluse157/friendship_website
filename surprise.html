A LETTER ACROSS THE DISTANCE

For you, my favorite kitty.

From Pakistan 🇵🇰 to Dubai 🇦🇪, here's a little piece of my heart written down instead of sent as just another message.

🐾 ♡ 🐾
FOR MY FAVORITE PERSON 💌
🐱

"If I could put every little thought I've had about us into one place, it would probably still need another hundred pages."

with love 🐾
A LITTLE NOTE 🐾
To my favorite kitty, ♡

Hey you... 🐱🤍

I honestly don't know how to start a letter like this because there are so many things I want to say. If I tried sending all of them in one normal message, I'd probably end up writing forever.

So I thought I'd put them here instead. A little place where I don't have to worry about making the message too long, because let's be honest... this letter was never going to be short.

You became someone really special to me in a way I didn't expect. Somewhere between our conversations, random messages, silly moments, busy days and those little windows where we manage to talk... you became part of my everyday thoughts.

“

Different places don't have to mean different hearts.

🐾

You're in Dubai. I'm here in Pakistan. There are miles between us, different routines and days when both of us are simply too busy.

But somehow, we still find each other.

And I really like that.

I like knowing that even when we're both busy doing our own things, there's still that little thought in the back of my mind...

"I should tell her this."

Or...

"She would probably laugh at this."

Or, obviously...

"I need to send her this kitty." 🐱

THE BUSY DAYS

I don't need every minute.

I know we both have our own work, responsibilities and things that need our attention.

I don't expect us to disappear from our own lives just to talk all day.

Actually, one of the things I appreciate most is that we can both have our own lives and still make room for each other.

Sometimes it's a long conversation. Sometimes it's only a few messages. Sometimes one of us replies later.

But the little effort is still there.

And that means more than constantly being online ever could.

😽
VERY IMPORTANT INFORMATION

You're kind of stuck with the kitty energy now.

I'm sorry, but once someone becomes part of a kitty-themed website, there is absolutely no escape.

You will receive random kitty pictures. You will receive silly messages. You may receive completely unnecessary affection. And you will probably have to tolerate my dramatic "where are youuu" moments. 🤭🐾

Consider yourself warned. 😼🤍

WHAT I APPRECIATE

It's the way we keep finding time.

I appreciate the little things.

The messages. The jokes. The random conversations. The moments when one of us is busy but still checks in.

I appreciate that our connection doesn't have to be perfect to be meaningful.

We can have busy days. We can have quiet days. We can have moments where there isn't much to say.

And still care.

🐾 ♡ 🐾
SOMEDAY

I hope the distance doesn't always stay this big.

I don't know exactly what the future will look like. Nobody really does.

But I do know there are things I'd love for us to experience someday.

More conversations. More memories. More ridiculous kitty moments. More reasons to smile.

And, when life gives us the opportunity, I hope there comes a time when the distance between Dubai and Pakistan feels a little less important than it does now.

Until then, we'll keep making our little moments.

LITTLE FUTURE WISHES

I have a few little wishes for our future.

I hope we keep talking even when life gets busier.

I hope we keep sending each other random things that make absolutely no sense to anyone else.

I hope we keep laughing at the dumbest jokes.

I hope there are many more nights where we realise we've been talking for way longer than we planned.

I hope there are more memories, more stories, more little surprises and more reasons to say,

"I'm really glad I have you."

And maybe one day, when the distance isn't the thing separating our days, we'll look back at all these messages and realise how much of our story started through a screen.

🌙 WISH 01

More late conversations

The kind where "just five minutes" somehow turns into a whole conversation.

🐾
📸 WISH 02

More little memories

More moments that we'll eventually look back at and smile about.

🐾
✨ WISH 03

More reasons to smile

Even on the busiest days, I hope we keep finding tiny reasons to make each other smile.

🐾
A FEW PROMISES

Not huge dramatic promises. Just little real ones.

I don't want to promise things that sound perfect only because they're written beautifully.

I'd rather promise the simple things that actually matter.

01 🤍

I'll respect your busy days.

I won't expect you to stop everything just because I want to talk. Your life matters too.

🐾
02 💬

I'll keep communicating.

When something matters, I'll try to talk about it instead of letting silence make everything complicated.

🐾
03 🐱

I'll keep the kitty energy alive.

This one is extremely serious. Random kitty pictures are basically a lifetime commitment.

🐾
04 🌷

I'll appreciate the little things.

I won't take the little moments for granted just because they're small.

🐾
🐾
THE SOFTER PART

You really are one of my favorite people.

There's something really sweet about having someone you can think about during an ordinary day.

Not because something huge happened. Just because a random thought appears and suddenly you want to tell them.

That's you sometimes.

I'll be doing something completely unrelated and suddenly think,

"She would definitely have something to say about this." 🤭

And yes... you have officially become one of those people whose opinion randomly appears in my head.

That's pretty cute, isn't it? 🐱🤍

😼
VERY SERIOUS FLIRTING DEPARTMENT

A tiny warning for you...

You should probably know that being this cute and then expecting me to behave normally is slightly unfair.

And yes, I'm aware that I just blamed you for my own inability to stop being cheesy. 🤭

Also, if you ever wonder why there are so many cute things on this website...

It's because apparently one cute person inspired an entire kitty universe. 🐱🐾

WHEN I MISS THE CONNECTION

Some days the distance feels a little louder.

There will probably be days when I wish we could just talk without checking schedules or waiting for a free moment.

Days when I wish the screen wasn't the thing connecting two different places.

But even then, I don't want the distance to make me forget how special the connection itself is.

Because being apart doesn't erase the conversations we've had.

It doesn't erase the laughs.

It doesn't erase the little moments.

And it definitely doesn't erase how much I care.

🐾

If distance ever feels annoying, we'll just blame the map.

The map is clearly the problem. 😭🐱

🐾
WHEN I THINK ABOUT OUR FUTURE

I don't know every detail, but I know what I hope for.

I hope life gives both of us beautiful opportunities. I hope we grow, learn, work hard and become better versions of ourselves.

And while we're busy building our own futures, I hope we never completely lose these little moments that belong to us.

I hope there are more birthdays, more celebrations, more random conversations and more reasons to look back and smile.

I hope one day we can look at the distance between Dubai and Pakistan and say,

"Remember when this used to feel so far away?" 🐾

🐱
MY LITTLE WISH LIST

Things I'd love for our story to have someday.

  • 🐾 More memories to look back on.
  • 🐾 More reasons to laugh together.
  • 🐾 More little surprises.
  • 🐾 More days where we make time despite being busy.
  • 🐾 More dreams we can talk about.
  • 🐾 And hopefully, someday, less distance between us.
MY FINAL PROMISES

A few things I want you to remember.

I promise I'll keep appreciating the little things.

I promise I'll try to communicate honestly when something matters.

I promise I'll respect your time, your work, your responsibilities and your own space.

I promise I'll keep cheering for the things you want to achieve.

And whenever life gets busy, I'll remember that being busy doesn't mean someone stopped caring.

Sometimes it simply means we're both trying to build our futures.

🐾 ♡ 🐾
😽

And I'll always be grateful for the little moments we get.

Because sometimes a few minutes with someone you care about can mean more than hours of ordinary conversation with anyone else.

— from your very slightly dramatic kitty 🐾
BEFORE I END THIS LETTER...

Thank you for being part of my little world.

Thank you for the conversations.

Thank you for the random moments.

Thank you for making time when you could have simply stayed busy with everything else.

Thank you for being someone I can think about during an ordinary day and suddenly smile.

I don't know exactly where every road ahead will take us.

But I know I'm happy that this little part of the journey exists.

And I'm really, really glad you're in my story. 🤍🐱

🐱
🐾 🐾 🐾

Until the next little chapter...

Stay cute, my favorite kitty. 🤍

And yes, you are absolutely responsible for at least 37% of the kitty content on this website. 😭🐾
